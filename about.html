About Me

I'm a 3rd-year BSIT student at Rizal Technological University (batch 2028). My interest in IT started with hands-on tinkering at home — troubleshooting our family computer, upgrading it from Windows 7 to 10, setting up our printer, and converting an old router into a wireless access point. Those small, practical fixes were what sparked my curiosity about how computers and networks actually work.

Coding isn't where my strongest interest lies, though I'm building a solid foundation in it since it's a core part of the BSIT curriculum. What genuinely pulls me in is the hardware and networking side of computing — how devices communicate, how systems are configured, and how problems get diagnosed and fixed at that level. I'm also exploring data-related work that leans more on structure and logic than heavy programming, like working with SQL and databases.

My goal for the rest of my studies is to build real, practical skills in networking and hardware — the kind that would prepare me for a role as a Network Engineer, while keeping data engineering open as path I'm still exploring.
